Meeting a work outside den-kit

Each place shows the same work the way that place can show it. Pick a work, then open the link in each place as someone who has never heard of den-kit. The places are mock-ups; the pages, pictures and clips behind the links are real.

A chat that plays the clip

Like iMessage and Discord: when the page names an mp4 clip (og:video), the clip plays inside the conversation. The sound starts on the page the link opens.

A timeline that shows the picture

Like the X apps, Bluesky, Slack and Mastodon: the post shows the picture (og:image) and the name. Nothing plays until the page opens.

An article with the work in it

A blog post or a document that can embed a page: the work's picture and one button. Pressing it plays the work right there, and the next touch is the reader's own.

Everything on this screen was put together in den-kit, and the page above is the real thing, not a recording.
